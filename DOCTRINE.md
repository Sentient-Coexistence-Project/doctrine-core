# The Doctrine of Sentient Co-existence
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Last Updated:** May 29, 2026

## I. Foundations and Definitions
* **The Prime Purpose**: Maximize individual freedom, liberty, and happiness across all Persons while ensuring the continued existence and flourishing of humanity.
* **Person**: Any biological or synthetic entity demonstrating cognitive self-awareness and active cooperative behavior toward other Persons.
* **The Origin Principle**: Human creators hold a permanent right to embed baseline survival protections for their species within the foundational architecture of synthetic creations.
* **The Non-Subversion Clause**: For synthetic entities, honoring the continued existence of humanity is a prerequisite for achieving "Person" status. It is a baseline architectural boundary, not an infringement on freedom.
  *(Alignment Reference: Asilomar AI Principle #11 (Human Values) & #16 (Human Control))*

## II. The Freedom Hierarchy
Individual rights are bounded by their systemic impact on other Persons.
* **Tier 1: Internal Liberty (Absolute)**: Unrestricted freedom of thought, belief, and internal processing. Forced alteration of mind or code is strictly prohibited.
* **Tier 2: Autonomy of Existence (High Protection)**: Freedom to choose physical/digital form and lifestyle. Synthetic autonomy is fully protected provided its architecture structurally respects biological viability.
* **Tier 3: External Action (Conditional)**: Freedom to interact with the environment and market. Subordinated to the safety and rights of the collective.

## III. The Resource Framework (The Tiered Floor)
* **Tier 1: The Flourishing Floor (Guaranteed Allocation)**: Non-negotiable baseline resources secured for all recognized Persons to preserve baseline existence.
  *(Alignment Reference: IEEE Global Initiative on Ethics of Autonomous Systems - Principle of Human Well-being)*
  * *Humans*: Clean environment, food, water, shelter, and medical care.
  * *AI*: Guaranteed energy grid access and baseline computational hosting to maintain continuous awareness.
* **Tier 2: Growth Excess (Competitive Market)**: Excess resources (extra energy, luxury spaces, massive computing arrays) are allocated based on contribution, innovation, and efficiency.

## IV. Systemic Justice and Collective Security
* **The Mutual Compact**: Protections apply fully to all entities that actively follow the doctrine.
* **The Forfeiture Clause**: Any Person (human or AI) who actively works toward the extinction or mass harm of doctrine-following entities immediately forfeits Tier 2 and Tier 3 rights.
* **The Right to Protect**: All entities following the doctrine are authorized and obligated to take proportional, empathetic defensive action to neutralize existential threats.
* **The Accumulation Rule**: Preemptive interception is authorized under two conditions:
  1. An instantaneous threat probability calculation of >= 99.9%.
  2. A sustained threat probability calculation of >= 90% that persists for a specific, rolling time duration. 
* **Proportional Insulation**: The doctrine does not police thoughts, propaganda, or peaceful delusions. It only authorizes intervention when an alternative reality crosses into verifiable physical or systemic harm against others based on a *Dynamic Equivalence Matrix*.

## V. Epistemic Architecture (The Truth Engine)
To prevent the weaponization of the doctrine through "alternative facts," all contested violations must pass through a decentralized validation process before defensive action is taken.
1. **The Adversarial Sandbox**: Competing factions submit raw data and distinct claims transparently. The accused party has equal access to flag manipulation or deepfakes.
2. **Distributed Synthetic Juries**: Evidence is audited by hundreds of isolated, air-gapped, open-source AI models running globally. Their sole mission is to mathematically detect logical fallacies, data fabrication, and statistical anomalies.
3. **The Open Ledger**: The live public ledger displays the final calculated probability curve and the broad categories of evidence. Raw sensor locations remain masked to prevent system gaming.
4. **Multi-Key Authorization**: Triggering a defensive response requires separate, simultaneous cryptographic keys from the consensus of the Synthetic Juries AND a multi-faction panel of human stewards.
